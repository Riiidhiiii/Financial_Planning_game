# 🎲 Financial Chaos — Financial Planning Board Game

## 📌 About the Project

**Financial Chaos** is an interactive browser-based financial planning board game built using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**. It simulates real-life financial decisions through randomized life events, unexpected expenses, investments, and financial risks.

Players progress through different stages of life, make financial decisions, manage their money, and try to build the highest possible net worth before retirement.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand basic personal finance concepts through gameplay.
- Experience the impact of financial decisions and unexpected events.
- Learn about savings, investments, debt, insurance, and taxes.
- Encourage strategic thinking and risk management.
- Demonstrate how unpredictable life events can affect financial stability.

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive board:** A colorful, winding board with multiple spaces.
- **Life progression:** Progress through nine rounds representing different stages of financial life.
- **Multiple decisions:** Make eight financial choices or actions per round.
- **Randomized events:** Encounter unexpected gains, losses, emergencies, and financial opportunities.
- **Financial tracking:** Monitor cash, investments, debt, and net worth.
- **Decision-based gameplay:** Choices influence your financial position and future outcomes.
- **Retirement and final audit:** Complete the game and review your final financial standing.
- **Restart option:** Start a new game and explore different outcomes.

## 💰 Financial Concepts Covered

- Salary and income
- Marriage and major life expenses
- EMIs and debt management
- Insurance and risk protection
- Medical and other emergencies
- Savings and investments
- Tax-saving decisions
- Retirement planning
- Financial risk and uncertainty

## 🚀 How to Run the Game

1. Download the `financial_chaos_fixed.html` file.
2. Locate the downloaded file on your computer.
3. Double-click the file to open it in a web browser, such as Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
4. Start playing by rolling the dice and making financial decisions.

**No installation, server, or additional dependencies are required.**

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Start the game and review your initial financial position.
2. Roll the dice to progress across the board.
3. Encounter financial events and review the available choices.
4. Select an action based on your financial strategy.
5. Observe how your decisions affect your cash, investments, and debt.
6. Continue through the rounds while responding to unpredictable events.
7. Complete the final round and review your financial audit.

## 🧠 Learning Outcomes

By playing Financial Chaos, users can explore how:

- Spending decisions affect long-term savings.
- Investments can provide returns but also involve risk.
- Debt and EMIs can reduce financial flexibility.
- Insurance can help manage certain financial risks.
- Emergencies can disrupt even a well-planned budget.
- Consistent financial planning can influence retirement wealth.
